## Verbatim prompt

Design a **footwear brand**. The website must look like it was designed by a top-tier international creative agency, not generated from a generic AI website template.

### BRAND DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated, modern footwear brand positioned between **luxury fashion and contemporary streetwear**. The brand should feel confident, minimal, desirable, premium and authentic.

Use a restrained visual identity with:

* Strong editorial art direction
* Sophisticated typography
* Generous whitespace
* Precise grid alignment
* Large, immersive product photography
* Subtle micro-interactions
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Minimal use of decorative elements

Avoid anything that looks like a generic SaaS landing page.

### HOMEPAGE

Create a cinematic hero section occupying most of the first viewport.

Hero:

* Full-width, ultra-realistic editorial photograph of a premium sneaker
* Shoe photographed like a professional fashion campaign
* Natural shadows and realistic materials
* Detailed leather/mesh/suede textures
* Sophisticated studio or architectural environment
* Dramatic but believable lighting
* Minimal background
* Large, elegant headline
* Short supporting statement
* One primary CTA: "Shop Collection"
* Secondary understated text link: "Explore the Story"

The shoe must be the visual focal point.

Do NOT use floating cards, excessive gradients, glowing effects, blobs, random shapes, glassmorphism or excessive animations.

### NAVIGATION

Create a refined navigation bar:

* Brand logo on the left
* Shop
* Men
* Women
* New Arrivals
* Collections
* About
* Search icon
* Account icon
* Shopping bag icon

Navigation should be clean, compact and editorial.

Use a transparent navigation over the hero initially, transitioning subtly to a solid background when scrolling.

### FEATURED COLLECTION

Immediately below the hero, create an editorial product section.

Show 3–4 premium shoes in a carefully designed grid.

Each product card should contain:

* Large realistic product image
* Product name
* Short descriptor
* Price
* Available colour indicator
* Subtle hover interaction

On hover:

* Product image transitions smoothly
* A second product angle may appear
* "Quick View" appears subtly

Keep the cards visually clean and spacious.

### EDITORIAL BRAND SECTION

Create a large split-screen editorial section combining:

* High-fashion lifestyle photography
* Brand story
* Large typography
* Short paragraph
* "Discover the Collection" CTA

Photography should show the shoes being worn naturally in a sophisticated urban environment.

The section should feel like a **fashion magazine editorial**, not a conventional e-commerce banner.

### PRODUCT DETAIL EXPERIENCE

Create a premium product page.

Layout:

* Large image gallery occupying approximately 60% of the screen
* Product information on the opposite side
* Product name
* Price
* Short product description
* Colour selection
* Size selector
* Size guide
* Quantity
* Large "Add to Bag" button
* Wishlist icon
* Shipping information
* Expandable details for Materials, Fit, Care and Delivery

Product photography should include:

1. Front view
2. Side profile
3. Rear view
4. Top view
5. Close-up material detail
6. Lifestyle photograph

Images must look like professional commercial footwear photography.

### INTERACTION DESIGN

Use subtle premium micro-interactions:

* Smooth page transitions
* Image zoom on product hover
* Elegant button hover states
* Smooth navigation transformation while scrolling
* Subtle text reveal animations
* Horizontal product scrolling where appropriate
* Smooth cart drawer
* Refined loading transitions

Animations must be restrained and fast.

The design should feel expensive because of **spacing, typography, photography and composition**, not because of excessive animation.

### TYPOGRAPHY

Use a sophisticated combination of:

* Modern grotesk/sans-serif for navigation and body text
* Elegant editorial serif or distinctive display typeface for major headlines

Typography should be large, confident and carefully spaced.

Avoid generic fonts and excessive font weights.

### COLOUR SYSTEM

Use a sophisticated neutral palette:

* Warm off-white
* Deep charcoal/black
* Soft grey
* Natural leather tones
* One restrained brand accent

Do not use bright gradients.

### FOOTER

Create a substantial editorial footer containing:

* Logo
* Newsletter signup
* Shop links
* Customer care
* About
* Social links
* Shipping information
* Returns
* Privacy
* Terms
* Country/currency selector

### VISUAL QUALITY

The final design must have:

* Photorealistic footwear
* Realistic materials
* Professional fashion photography
* Natural shadows
* High-resolution imagery
* Consistent product photography
* Realistic proportions
* Premium typography
* Sophisticated spacing
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Pixel-perfect alignment
* Responsive behaviour across desktop, tablet and mobile

### IMPORTANT — AVOID AI-LOOKING DESIGN

Do NOT produce:

* Generic AI landing page layouts
* Excessive rounded cards
* Huge gradient backgrounds
* Purple/blue AI gradients
* Floating glassmorphism cards
* Random decorative 3D objects
* Excessive shadows
* Excessive border-radius
* Stock-photo-looking imagery
* Generic sneaker mockups
* Overly complicated navigation
* Too many sections
* Excessive animations
* Unnecessary icons
* Cluttered layouts
* Template-like design

The final result should resemble a **real premium footwear brand such as an independent luxury sneaker label**, with the quality of a professional fashion e-commerce website.

Prioritise **art direction, typography, product photography, whitespace, composition and usability over decorative effects**.

Make every section feel intentional and commercially realistic.

The final website should look like something that could genuinely launch as a premium footwear brand in 2026.
